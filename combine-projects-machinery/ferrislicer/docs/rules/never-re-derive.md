# CLAUDE.md § Never re-derive — full rule

A fact is computed ONCE, at its authority, and every consumer READS it. Nothing
downstream recomputes a private opinion of a fact that already exists — not a
pipeline stage, not an emitter, not a parser, not a probe.

- Pipeline: stages read the authority's table (`SolidLadder`, `ExposedSurfaces`,
  the wall pass's deposited footprints) — "read its rung rather than deriving a
  private opinion of it." A second derivation WILL disagree with the first;
  every campaign this window found that disagreement wearing a defect's face.
- Formats: if a consumer must infer a fact from arithmetic (wipe-ness from the
  sign of E), the format is wrong — the producer states the fact explicitly
  (`;WIPE_START`/`;WIPE_END`). An inferable encoding is a re-derivation forced
  on every reader forever.
- Probes: dump the binding the pipeline actually uses, never a recomputed
  "equivalent" — three ground-shaping attempts were judged against a set no
  stage ever saw.
- Classification: generation-time classification is authoritative; annotations
  flow from it (thin sections, overhangs, bridges). Consumers never re-classify.

**The rule governs the PRODUCTION path, and INVERTS in a test's expected value**
(Gabe, 2026-08-17). Inside the pipeline a second opinion is a defect. On the
right-hand side of an assertion a second opinion is the entire point — a test
whose expected value came from the engine cannot report that the engine is
wrong, only that it changed.

- Read the engine for the MEASUREMENT (`path.width`, `loop.role`, the deposited
  footprints). Never reconstruct those from coordinates or from E; that half of
  the rule stands unchanged.
- Derive the EXPECTATION from something the engine never produced: the config
  the axis states, the dimension the fixture states, the mesh's own cross
  section, or arithmetic (`E/mm = bead area x flow / filament area`). Both sides
  from the engine is self-consistency, and a uniform error passes it.
- A number pasted from a probe run is a REGRESSION PIN, not a test. Pins are
  legitimate and useful on a deterministic engine — every delta is signal — but
  they are labelled as pins and never counted as coverage.
- Where an expectation genuinely cannot be derived yet, say so at the assertion
  site and leave the work undone rather than pinning the engine's current answer
  and calling it a contract.

## Evidence and history

From the "regression pin" bullet (the pin taxonomy stays in CLAUDE.md):

  Measured on GIT_345's
  catalog (2026-08-17): of 30 contract checks, 12 carried expectations that were
  probe literals; the other 18 were behavioural (deposit-or-warn, warning codes)
  or topological (a donut has two contours) and were sound as written. The
  literals also silently locked their entries to the one axis they were measured
  at — seven wave-1 entries ran only at N04 for no reason anyone had decided.
